 SkillPath AI — Your Career, Your Roadmap

SkillPath AI is a career-preparation web platform concept designed to help college students navigate internship and placement preparation through structured learning roadmaps, company exploration, and role-specific guidance.

The platform helps students identify their career goals, choose target companies, and organize their preparation according to their current skill level, academic background, programming language, and available study time.

 Features

- Personalized Learning Roadmaps — Configure your desired career role, technical level, and daily study schedule.
- Company Exploration — Explore leading companies such as Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Apple, Meta, and more.
- Role-Based Career Paths — Discover preparation paths for Software Engineering, AI/ML, Data Science, Cybersecurity, Cloud Engineering, and Full-Stack Development.
- Targeted Placement Preparation — Select a target company and hiring pathway to customize your preparation priorities.
- Company Categories — Browse opportunities across product companies, IT services, consulting, AI and data, and cloud and enterprise software.
- Student-Focused Learning — Organize career preparation into manageable steps based on individual goals and skill levels.

Problem Statement

Students often struggle to determine which skills to learn, which technologies to prioritize, and how to prepare for their desired companies. SkillPath AI aims to make this process more organized by bringing career exploration and preparation guidance together in one platform.
